# Personas

1. **The IELTS Candidate (Primary)**
   - **Demographics:** Individual looking to study for IELTS on-the-go.
   - **Goals:** Requires a robust portal to watch learning videos, read dense curriculum PDFs, and listen to test audio tracks efficiently.
   - **Pain Points:** Needs audio playback to seamlessly pause/resume and allow accurate seeking. Needs PDFs to zoom naturally and not lose tracking when navigating between apps.

2. **Backend Services (System)**
   - **Behavior:** Remote data endpoints supplying the `MediaItem` configurations telling the `ocg_ielts` app which URLs/Assets to load into the `Riverpod` player states.
